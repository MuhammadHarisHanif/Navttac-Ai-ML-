# Azure AI Service Selection

## 1. Objective

Understand how to select appropriate Microsoft Azure AI services for different types of AI solutions.

## 2. Service Selection

| Requirement | Azure AI Area | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Image Analysis | Azure AI Vision | Analyze images and identify visual information |
| Language Analysis | Azure AI Language | Analyze and understand text |
| Speech Processing | Azure AI Speech | Convert speech to text and text to speech |
| Decision Support | Azure AI Decision | Support decision-making and anomaly detection |
| Document Processing | Azure AI Document Intelligence | Extract information from documents |
| Conversational AI | Azure AI Services / Azure AI Foundry | Build intelligent conversational applications |

## 3. Practical Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Image Analysis

**Problem:** A company wants to analyze uploaded images.

**Selected Area:** Azure AI Vision

**Expected Result:** Extract useful information from images.

### Scenario 2: Language Analysis

**Problem:** A business wants to analyze customer feedback.

**Selected Area:** Azure AI Language

**Expected Result:** Identify sentiment, key phrases and important information.

### Scenario 3: Speech Processing

**Problem:** A company wants to convert recorded speech into text.

**Selected Area:** Azure AI Speech

**Expected Result:** Generate text from spoken language.

### Scenario 4: Decision Support

**Problem:** A company wants to identify unusual patterns in its data.

**Selected Area:** Azure AI services for anomaly detection.

**Expected Result:** Identify unusual data patterns.

## 4. Conclusion

Different Azure AI services are designed for different requirements. Selecting the correct service depends on the problem, input data and expected output.

## 5. Learning Outcome

After completing this exercise, trainees should understand how to identify suitable Azure AI services for Vision, Language, Speech and Decision Support solutions.
