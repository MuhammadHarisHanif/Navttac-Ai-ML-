# Practical 1: Video Processing with Microsoft Azure AI

## Objective
Understand how video processing solutions work and how AI can be used to process video content.

## 1. Introduction to Video Processing

Video processing involves analyzing video files to extract useful information.

AI-based video processing can help identify:
- Objects and activities in video frames.
- Important scenes and events.
- Visual information from recorded videos.
- Useful information for video analysis.

## 2. Practical Scenario

**Example:** A security monitoring system processes recorded videos to help identify important activities and events.

## 3. General Video Processing Workflow

1. Select a sample video file.
2. Upload or provide the video to a suitable video processing service.
3. Configure the required analysis.
4. Process the video.
5. Review the analysis results.
6. Extract useful information from the processed video.

## 4. Applications

- Security and surveillance.
- Traffic monitoring.
- Video content analysis.
- Media and entertainment.
- Industrial monitoring.

## Learning Outcome

After completing this practical, I understand the basic purpose of video processing and how AI-based solutions can analyze video content.

## Conclusion

Video processing allows AI systems to analyze visual information from recorded videos and extract useful insights.
