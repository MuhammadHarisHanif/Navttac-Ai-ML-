# Practical 4: Knowledge Mining with Microsoft Azure AI

## Objective
Understand how knowledge mining solutions extract useful information from large collections of documents and data.

## 1. Introduction to Knowledge Mining

Knowledge mining uses AI to discover, organize, and extract useful information from different data sources.

It can help organizations search large amounts of information and identify relevant insights.

## 2. Important Concepts

### Data Ingestion
Collecting information from different sources, such as documents, files, and databases.

### Data Enrichment
Using AI techniques to extract additional information from collected data.

### Indexing
Organizing extracted information so it can be searched efficiently.

### Search
Finding relevant information using keywords or queries.

## 3. Practical Scenario

**Example:** A company has thousands of PDF documents containing policies, reports, and employee records.

A knowledge mining solution can help employees search these documents and find relevant information quickly.

## 4. General Knowledge Mining Workflow

1. Collect documents and data from different sources.
2. Connect the data sources to a suitable AI search solution.
3. Configure data ingestion.
4. Apply AI enrichment to extract useful information.
5. Create a searchable index.
6. Search the indexed information.
7. Review the results and identify relevant information.

## 5. Applications

- Enterprise document search.
- Research and knowledge management.
- Business intelligence.
- Document analysis.
- Information retrieval.
- Organizational knowledge systems.

## Learning Outcome

After completing this practical, I understand the basic knowledge mining process, including data ingestion, enrichment, indexing, and searching.

## Conclusion

Knowledge mining helps organizations convert large collections of unstructured information into searchable and useful knowledge.
