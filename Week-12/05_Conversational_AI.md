# Practical 5: Conversational AI with Microsoft Azure

## Objective
Understand the concepts and implementation workflow of conversational AI solutions.

## 1. Introduction to Conversational AI

Conversational AI enables computers to communicate with users through natural language using text or voice.

It allows applications to understand user questions and provide relevant responses.

**Examples:**
- Customer support chatbots.
- Virtual assistants.
- Banking chatbots.
- E-commerce assistants.
- Educational assistants.

## 2. Important Components

### Natural Language Understanding (NLU)
Identifies the user's intention and extracts relevant information from messages.

### Dialogue Management
Controls the conversation flow and determines the next response or action.

### Knowledge Base
Stores information that an AI assistant can use to answer questions.

### Response Generation
Creates or selects an appropriate response for the user.

## 3. Practical Scenario

**Example:** An educational institution develops an AI chatbot to answer student questions.

A student asks:

"What are the admission requirements?"

The chatbot identifies the question and provides the relevant information from its knowledge source.

## 4. General Conversational AI Workflow

1. Identify the purpose of the chatbot.
2. Define common user questions and intents.
3. Prepare the required knowledge source.
4. Configure language understanding.
5. Design conversation flows.
6. Connect the chatbot to the required services.
7. Test the chatbot with different questions.
8. Evaluate responses and improve the conversation experience.

## 5. Applications

- Customer service automation.
- Student helpdesks.
- Online shopping assistance.
- Appointment booking.
- Banking support.
- Business information systems.

Conversational AI allows businesses and organizations to automate communication and provide users with interactive assistance through text and voice.
